LEGISLATIVE COUNSEL'S DIGEST
ACR 77, Example. Social Worker Appreciation Month.
This measure would proclaim March as Social Worker Appreciation Month.
WHEREAS, Social workers serve California families with dedication; now, therefore, be it
Resolved by the Assembly of the State of California, the Senate thereof concurring, That the Legislature proclaims March as Social Worker Appreciation Month; and be it further
Resolved, That the Chief Clerk of the Assembly transmit copies of this resolution to the author for appropriate distribution.

LEGISLATIVE COUNSEL'S DIGEST
AB 999, as amended, Example. Public social services: reporting.
This bill would require the State Department of Social Services to report annually to the Legislature on program outcomes.
THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA DO ENACT AS FOLLOWS:
SECTION 1. Section 11450.5 is added to the Welfare and Institutions Code, to read:
11450.5. (a) The department shall report annually to the Legislature on program outcomes, beginning January 1, 2028.
(b) The department shall notify each recipient in writing of the change no later than July 1, 2027.
(c) The department shall share eligibility data with each county & the Franchise Tax Board through a secure interface.
